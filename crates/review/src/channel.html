What's on top of the suit filter?

How to mark

Play the clip, then pick what you hear on top of her usual suit sound. Plain means just her normal suit voice. Radio means a radio or comms effect. Other effect is anything else added (echo, muffling, distortion, a different room). Can't tell is fine. Saving needs a play first. You can go back and change any clip; the last mark counts. Every mark is written to channel.jsonl immediately.
